# Design — TK Mobile Service

## Genre

Editorial repair atelier: calm, precise and proof-led rather than promotional.

## Macrostructure family

- Marketing pages: Workbench evidence sequence; real workshop and repair evidence lead the page.
- Service pages: diagnosis-first long-form reference with clear actions.
- Content pages: readable guide with short, direct answers.

## Theme

Warm ivory paper, charcoal ink and a restrained amber accent. See `tokens.css` for the locked OKLCH values.

## Typography

- Display: Noto Serif Thai, 500, upright only.
- Body: Manrope + Noto Sans Thai, 400–800.
- Display uses `--text-display`; body copy is never below `--text-sm` for primary reading.

## Motion

Quiet button feedback only: background colour and a 1px press translation. No scroll reveals, looping animation, or animated backgrounds. Reduced motion is instant.

## CTA voice

- Primary: charcoal filled, square-soft corners, direct action.
- Secondary: transparent hairline outline.

## Evidence rule

Use actual shop, bench and repair photos whenever available. Any AI image stays labelled as an illustration and never represents a real repair result.

## What every page shares

The wordmark, ivory paper, charcoal ink, amber only for emphasis, hairline dividers, readable Thai copy, and calm single-effect interactions.
